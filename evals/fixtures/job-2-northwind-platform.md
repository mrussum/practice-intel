# Platform Engineer
Company: Northwind Cloud

Northwind Cloud runs a multi-tenant data platform. The platform team owns the Kubernetes estate, CI/CD and developer tooling for 80 engineers.

What you'll do
- Operate and evolve our Kubernetes clusters across three regions.
- Build golden paths so product teams can ship safely without tickets.
- Own observability: metrics, tracing and alerting.

Requirements
- Kubernetes: operating production clusters (EKS or GKE), including upgrades and incident response
- Terraform: managing cloud infrastructure as code
- AWS: networking, IAM and managed services
- Go: writing operators, controllers or internal tooling
- Observability: Prometheus, Grafana or OpenTelemetry in production
- CI/CD: designing pipelines used by many teams

Nice to have
- Docker: building minimal, secure images
- PostgreSQL: operating managed databases
- Helm: packaging services for Kubernetes

Benefits
Hybrid (2 days in Manchester), private healthcare, on-call allowance.

# Machine Learning Engineer
Company: Lumen Health

Lumen Health builds models that help clinicians triage patient referrals. You will take models from research notebooks to monitored production services.

What you'll do
- Productionise and monitor clinical risk models.
- Build training and evaluation pipelines with our research scientists.
- Work within strict data-governance and audit requirements.

Requirements
- Python: production ML code, packaging and testing
- PyTorch: training and fine-tuning deep learning models
- MLOps: model versioning, deployment and drift monitoring
- Statistics: experimental design, calibration and model evaluation
- Spark: large-scale data processing pipelines
- Healthcare data: working with sensitive data under GDPR or HIPAA

Nice to have
- Kubernetes: serving models at scale
- AWS: SageMaker or similar managed ML services

Benefits
Remote across Europe, equity, conference budget.
